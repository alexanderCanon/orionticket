Scrum, simplified.
Not full enterprise Scrum with all the ceremonies. A lightweight version that gives you the coordination benefit without the overhead.
Here's why Scrum fits:

4 people is exactly the size it was designed for
You have a defined product with clear scope (you just finished discovery)
You have a deadline (university project timeline)
The work can be broken into independent pieces (microservices help here)

Roles — keep it simple:

Product Owner	1 person — owns the backlog, decides priorities
Scrum Master	1 person — runs ceremonies, removes blockers
Developers
The other 2 (or all 4 rotate SM/PO responsibilities)

Ceremonies — only these four:

Sprint Planning — start of each sprint, decide what gets built
Daily Standup — 15 min max, three questions: what I did, what I'll do, what's blocking me
Sprint Review — end of sprint, demo what was built
Sprint Retrospective — what went well, what didn't, one thing to improve

Artifacts — three things:

Product Backlog — the full list of everything to build, prioritized
Sprint Backlog — what the team committed to this sprint only
Definition of Done — a written agreement on what "done" means for a task (coded + tested + reviewed + documented)
